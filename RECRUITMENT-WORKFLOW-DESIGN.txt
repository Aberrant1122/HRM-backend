Recruitment workflow and data design — 6 November 2026
Prepared from HRM Update register DOC-030–049 and roadmap phase 5. Design for product review; no recruitment migrations, permissions, public publishing, real resumes, invitations or integration calls have been implemented. Repository inventory found no existing recruitment/candidate/interview/onboarding module. Manual entry and reviewed imports can precede an external integration; Indeed availability is not assumed.

Required outcomes and proposed records
DOC-032–033: JobOpening has an internal immutable ID, title, description, draft/published/closed state, department/designation references when approved, publication timestamps and revision history. Internal publishing state and external-channel publication are separate. JobPublication records channel, external ID, last acknowledged revision, outcome/error and correlation key; do not label an external post published until acknowledged. Public careers page, approval owner and permitted fields need a product decision before public publishing.
DOC-034–035: Candidate is a recruiting identity with name, personal contact details and source/provenance, independent of employee account/login role. CandidateDocument holds private storage key, version, original name, type, size, hash, uploader and upload time. Keep old resume versions and explicit current version; no public storage URLs or attachment access inherited from an employee directory. Duplicate detection flags possible matches; it must not silently merge two people based on name/email. Deletion/retention is a separate decision.
DOC-036–038: Application links candidate to a job, with current reviewed/shortlisted/rejected disposition, current interview stage and optional reason. ApplicationEvent appends who changed what, prior/new values, timestamp and reason; historical events are not replaced by the current label. Interview has application, stage identifier/order, scheduled time with explicit zone/offset, interviewers and scheduled/completed/cancelled state. InterviewFeedback is attributed to an interview/author and versioned. No automatic stage advancement, rejection or employee performance score is inferred from feedback.
DOC-039,041: CompensationExpectation and Offer belong to the application, not the candidate globally. Store decimal amount, explicit currency, pay period and version; missing data is null, not zero. Offer stores proposed/sent/accepted/rejected/withdrawn outcome, actor, decision time and reason, with an immutable snapshot of the terms/letter version actually sent. This does not choose a tax rule or update payroll salary. Required reasons and permitted offer transitions need review.
DOC-040,043–049: OnboardingCase links the selected application, accepted offer version and eventual employee identity. ChecklistTemplate and ChecklistItem retain template version, assignee, due date, completion evidence and audit events. OfferLetterVersion retains editable draft, rendered artifact and sent version. Signed/accepted documents remain private and versioned. An approved conversion creates/links one employee and profile, assigns approved employee ID/department/designation/manager, records the application link and then requests an invitation. Account activation, offer acceptance and employment start are separate events.

Workflow boundary and commands
1. Create/edit job draft; validate description. Publish only to an approved channel/audience. Closing a job preserves applications and publication history.
2. Enter/import candidate and resume. Stage imports in a dry-run with row errors, source provenance, duplicate candidates and application conflicts. Commit reviewed rows with stable import-row IDs so retries do not duplicate records. CSV/XLSX schema, document import format and duplicate-resolution UI need product sign-off; no automatic inbox scraping.
3. Attach candidate to opening; review/shortlist/reject with attributed history. Stage changes use an expected version so a stale browser cannot silently overwrite another reviewer.
4. Schedule interview; capture attributed feedback. Store instants plus scheduling zone; until company zone is selected the UI requires an explicit zone rather than converting a guessed company 'today'. Interview notification delivery requires approved recipients and mail integration; local capture remains the development default.
5. Record expectation; draft/version offer and editable letter. Send only with approved authority and recipients. Record acceptance/rejection with reason. A candidate can have multiple applications and offers without changing other applications.
6. Start onboarding from the specifically selected offer/application after the approved acceptance gate. Complete/waive checklist items through the agreed authority. An accepted offer alone does not create an active account or establish an employment lifecycle.
7. Convert through a single transaction: lock application/onboarding row, verify expected version and conversion prerequisites, create/link approved employee/profile, persist unique conversion reference and an invitation-outbox entry. A repeated command returns the same result; no second employee or invitation. Existing email/employee-ID conflict returns a reviewable conflict and never auto-merges or overwrites an account. Send after commit via idempotent outbox; delivery failure leaves a retryable state, not a half-created second employee.

Relational integrity and migration outline for later implementation
Use foreign keys for candidate/application/job/interview/offer/onboarding ownership. Require one successful conversion per onboarding/application through a unique conversion key; employee linkage persists even if a role later changes. Do not cascade-delete employee or payroll history from recruitment. Use optimistic row versions for edits and transactional event/outbox inserts. Do not impose globally unique candidate email or one lifetime application per job until reapplication/duplicate policy is agreed. New schema would be additive; legacy users are not mass-imported into candidates or reclassified. Attachment authorization is checked before storage writes, and downloads re-check current account/capability against the owning application. Proposed list/detail APIs must allowlist fields rather than serialize whole models.

Permission and privacy dependency
The current system has admin/manager/employee/client login semantics. HR/Accounts capabilities have not been approved. No new HR/Accounts entitlement is invented here, and existing admin access is not assumed to satisfy future recruiting privacy. Product must name who can manage jobs, view resumes/contact data, see expectation/offer amounts, submit/read feedback, approve/send offers, convert hires and manage retention. Design endpoints around those capabilities after approval; candidate data must never enter public employee-list results. Logs contain IDs/outcomes, not resumes, compensation, tokens or private contact payloads.

Smallest product choices needed, in implementation order
A. First internal recruitment audience and capability owner: map the actions above to existing identities/groups; specify offer approval/send and hire-conversion authority. This is the first implementation gate for authenticated recruitment operations.
B. Application pipeline: permitted interview stages, review/shortlist/reject/reopen transitions, required rejection/offer reasons, reapplications and whether several live applications may exist for the same job/person. Keep disposition, interview stage and offer outcome separate.
C. Hiring handoff: exact onboarding prerequisites, employee ID format/source, duplicate email/account handling and whether personal-email entry saves a draft account, queues an invitation or enables login. DOC-048 requests creation but does not settle activation timing; do not auto-enable access.
D. Publication: internal only vs public careers page vs Indeed, publication approval, external account/API eligibility and permitted exposed fields. Manual candidate entry/import is independent; approved supported imports require a concrete field mapping/sample with synthetic rows. No external integration credentials or eligibility were verified.
E. Sensitive data retention and access audit; offer currency/pay period and template/letter signature process; interview scheduling/company reporting zone and notification recipients. None selects workforce membership, leave policy or payroll tax treatment.

Acceptance pack to implement after those decisions
Job draft/version/publication acknowledgement and safe channel failure; candidate with multiple applications; resume version/private download denial before storage; duplicate import dry-run and retry idempotency; stale pipeline-edit conflict; attributed interview feedback; offer revisions and accepted snapshot; concurrent/repeated conversion producing one employee/outbox entry; existing-email conflict with no overwrites; delivery retry after commit; closing job retains history; recruitment lists exclude unauthorized compensation/contact/document fields. Use synthetic local fixtures and capture mail. Separate production acceptance and any real-data import require release review.

Next buildable slice after decision A/B: additive job/candidate/application tables plus private manual-entry/list/detail and reviewed/shortlisted/rejected transitions, with migration/authorization/history tests. Offer/onboarding conversion and external publication wait for their actual dependencies rather than blocking manual-entry design.
